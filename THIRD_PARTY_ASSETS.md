# Third-party media

The weather background videos in `public/weather-videos/` are shortened, muted, cross-faded, and recompressed derivatives of the following Pexels videos:

- `clear.mp4`: [Clouds in Blue Sky](https://www.pexels.com/video/clouds-in-blue-sky-11868811/) by Dmitry Marchenkov
- `clouds.mp4`: [Time Lapse Footage of Dark Clouds](https://www.pexels.com/video/time-lapse-footage-of-dark-clouds-4489974/) by Artyom Saqib
- `rain.mp4`: [Rain over Window](https://www.pexels.com/video/rain-over-window-13107321/) by Yahia Alibrahim
- `snow.mp4`: [Snow Falling Video](https://www.pexels.com/video/snow-falling-video-1856985/) by Lay-Z Owl
- `night.mp4`: [Night Sky Timelapse](https://www.pexels.com/video/night-sky-timelapse-5837931/) by Dom Le Roy
